# Refund Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines NordShop's standard business rules for refunds arising from withdrawal and return cases.

It covers full refunds, partial refunds, conditions for releasing repayment, repayment timing, NordShop's internal refund-initiation target, refund payment method, bank and payment-provider processing time, and value reduction.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the standard NordShop refund process for withdrawal and return cases governed by the Returns Policy.

Refunds arising from other business processes, such as warranty, defect, damage, compensation, or other claims, are governed by separate NordShop processes and are not covered by this policy.

The Returns Policy defines the applicable withdrawal and return rules.

## 3. General Refund Rule

For a standard full withdrawal-based refund, NordShop refunds:

- the price of the goods covered by the withdrawal;
- the original NordShop Standard Delivery cost actually paid by the customer.

Applicable Standard Delivery pricing and free-shipping rules are defined in the Shipping Policy.

## 4. Partial Returns and Partial Refunds

Partial returns are permitted under the Returns Policy.

For an individual partial-return case, the concrete treatment of the original Standard Delivery cost, together with the final partial-refund calculation and approval, is handled through NordShop's authorized refund process.

## 5. When a Refund May Be Released

NordShop follows the applicable statutory refund rule.

Physical receipt of the returned goods is not always required before repayment can be released.

Where applicable, repayment may be released when NordShop has received either:

- the returned goods; or
- acceptable proof of dispatch.

If the available evidence requires an individual assessment, that assessment is handled through the applicable NordShop process.

## 6. Repayment Period

NordShop repays without undue delay and no later than 14 days after receiving the withdrawal declaration, subject to the applicable statutory right to withhold repayment until the returned goods or acceptable proof of dispatch have been received.

## 7. NordShop Refund Initiation Target

Once a refund can be released under the applicable refund rules, NordShop targets initiation of the refund within:

**3 NordShop business days.**

For this internal service target, NordShop business days are Monday through Friday.

This is a NordShop internal service target.

Where an applicable statutory repayment obligation requires earlier action, the statutory requirement takes precedence; the internal target must not extend it.

The internal target is not:

- a statutory deadline;
- a guarantee that the refunded amount will already be visible in the customer's bank account;
- a replacement for the applicable statutory repayment rules.

## 8. Refund Payment Method

Refunds are made to the original payment method.

A different payment method is not the standard NordShop refund path and may be used only where it has been explicitly agreed and does not cause additional cost to the customer.

## 9. Bank and Payment-Provider Processing Time

After NordShop has initiated a refund, the time until the refunded amount becomes visible to the customer may depend on the bank or payment provider.

NordShop does not promise a fixed bank-processing or payment-provider-processing time.

## 10. Value Reduction

A reduction in value may be legally relevant where the applicable statutory conditions are met.

Assessment of whether value reduction applies, any deduction, and the resulting refund amount is handled through the applicable NordShop review and refund process.

## 11. Authorized Refund Actions

Refund approval, initiation, modification, and execution are handled through NordShop's authorized refund process.

## 12. Relationship to Shipping and Returns

Shipping, returns, and refunds are related but separate business concepts.

The Shipping Policy governs:

- outbound Standard Delivery;
- outbound shipping pricing;
- the free-shipping threshold.

The Returns Policy governs:

- withdrawal rules;
- withdrawal declarations;
- physical return rules;
- the return procedure;
- return shipping.

This Refund Policy governs:

- repayment rules;
- treatment of the original outbound Standard Delivery cost;
- refund timing;
- refund payment method;
- refund-specific financial rules.

A completed physical return does not by itself mean that the refund has been completed.

## 13. Policy Boundaries

This policy does not define:

- withdrawal declaration requirements;
- physical return procedures;
- outbound shipping prices beyond the cross-reference to the Shipping Policy;
- warranty, defect, damage, or compensation refund processes;
- detailed bank or payment-provider processing rules;
- individual legal assessments beyond the standard rules stated here.

Those matters are governed by the applicable NordShop business policy or business process.
